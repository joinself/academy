## Key Principles

Our agreements are built on several core principles:

- **Transparency**: Clear, understandable language about how we operate
- **User Control**: You maintain control over your personal data
- **Security**: Robust protection for all data and communications
- **Compliance**: Adherence to relevant laws and regulations
- **Fairness**: Balanced terms that protect all parties
